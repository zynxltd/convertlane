### A. Corporate & identity

| # | Document | Notes |
|---|----------|--------|
| 1 | Certificate of incorporation | PDF; must match legal name on application |
| 2 | Memorandum / articles of association | Current version |
| 3 | Register of directors | Or extract from official registry |

| 6 | Proof of signatory authority | Board resolution or power of attorney if signatory is not sole director |

### B. Financial

| # | Document | Notes |
|---|----------|--------|
| 7 | Last filed **statutory accounts** | Less than 18 months old |
| 8 | **Management accounts** (if trading < 2 years) | Last 3 months; must show runway |
| 9 | **Credit check authorisation** (signed) | Attached — required before any unsecured payment terms |
| 10 | Bank reference letter **or** confirmation of ability to prepay | Finance may request prepay amount after review |

### C. Product & regulatory

| # | Document | Notes |
|---|----------|--------|
| 11 | **Advertiser due diligence questionnaire** (completed) | Attached |

| 13 | Live **landing page URL(s)** | Must match product described in application |
| 14 | Sample creatives (where available) | Banners, email, or ad copy samples |
| 15 | Privacy policy and terms & conditions | Links must work on live landing pages |

### D. Technical & commercial

| # | Document | Notes |
|---|----------|--------|
| 17 | **Postback / tracking specification** | Endpoint URL, parameters, test credentials |
| 18 | Draft **Insertion Order** | We will provide template if not yet shared |
| 19 | **Advertiser Agreement** (signed) | Master terms — sent separately or attached |

### E. Funding (read now — submit after commercial draft)

New advertisers: **prepay is required** before any offer goes live unless ConvertLane approves a **credit line** in writing.

- Minimum initial funding is typically **£5,000 / $5,000 / €5,000** or one month’s expected exposure (whichever is higher).  
- Final amount will be confirmed by Finance after review.  
- If you require **credit terms**, complete the **Credit Application** (available on request) with signed credit check authorisation.
