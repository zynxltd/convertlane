Dear {{contact_name}},

Thank you for applying to partner with **ConvertLane** as an advertiser.

We have received your application and opened reference **{{partner_id}}**. Our Compliance and Finance teams will review your company before any campaign can go live.

### What happens next

1. **Document request** — You will receive a separate email within **2 business days** listing required due diligence documents.  
2. **Review** — Once your pack is complete, we aim to provide a decision within **5 business days**.  
3. **Commercial setup** — If approved, you will execute our Advertiser Agreement and Insertion Order(s), and complete **funding** (prepay or approved credit) before traffic starts.

### Important information

- ConvertLane works with **corporate entities only** (not individual applicants).  
- New advertisers are typically required to **prepay** campaign funds before offers go live. Credit terms are granted only after review.  
- We do not provide tracking links or publisher access until status is **Approved** and funding is confirmed.
