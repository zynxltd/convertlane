# Finance vertical — advertiser targets

**For:** ConvertLane launch (loans, credit cards, credit score, personal finance)  
**Geo focus:** UK first, then US if you have US-licensed partners

Use this as an outreach list. Verify each company’s affiliate programme status and compliance before signing — programmes open/close and terms change.

---

## How to find and close finance advertisers

1. **Check Offervault / oDigger** — search “loans UK”, “credit card”, “credit score”. Note which **networks** already run the offer (competitive intel).
2. **LinkedIn** — search “Affiliate Manager” + company name, or “Partnerships” + fintech brand.
3. **Direct to brand** — many fintechs have “Partners” or “Affiliates” in footer (often Impact, Partnerize, or in-house).
4. **Lead-gen platforms** — companies that *are* the advertiser (they buy traffic and resell leads). Often easier to onboard than big banks.

**Your pitch:** Vetted affiliates only, prepay protection, Offer18 tracking, UK-registered network, IO-backed terms.

## Loans (UK)

| Type | Examples to approach | Typical model | Notes |
|------|---------------------|---------------|-------|
| Personal loans | **Zopa**, **Plend**, **Lendable** | CPA on funded loan | FCA-regulated — confirm affiliate programme exists |
| Comparison / aggregator | **MoneySuperMarket**, **Confused.com**, **GoCompare** | CPL / CPA | Often work through major networks; try partnerships team |
| Specialist / sub-prime | **Everyday Loans**, **118 118 Money** | CPL | Stricter compliance on ad copy |
| Business loans | **Funding Circle**, **iwoca** | CPA / CPL | B2B angle — different affiliate pool |
| Lead buyers (easier first deal) | **LeadPile**, **Ring Partner**, **Aragon** (verify current status) | CPL | They are the advertiser; you send qualified leads |

**Easiest first wins:** Lead-buying platforms and smaller FCA-authorised lenders with active performance budgets.

## Outreach priority (Week 1)

| Priority | Who | Why |
|----------|-----|-----|
| 1 | Credit score (ClearScore, TotallyMoney, Experian) | Clear event, affiliate-friendly, easier DD |
| 2 | Lead buyers (LeadPile-type platforms) | They want volume; fast IO |
| 3 | Smaller FCA lenders (Zopa, Plend) | Credible brand, performance budgets |
| 4 | Comparison sites (MSM, Uswitch) | High volume but slow procurement |
| 5 | Card issuers via Impact/Partnerize | Apply as publisher first to see rates, then pitch as network |

---

## What to ask on first call

1. Allowed traffic types (SEO, native, paid social, email)?
2. Payable event (application, approval, funded loan, first payment)?
3. Payout range and cap?
4. Postback / pixel spec for Offer18?
5. Prepay minimum to open caps?
6. FCA / compliance copy restrictions?
7. Existing network relationships (exclusivity)?

## Sample IO commercial starting points (negotiate)

| Vertical | Event | Typical pub payout (indicative) |
|----------|-------|--------------------------------|
| Credit score signup | SOI / free registration | £2–£8 UK |
| Credit card application | Approved application | £30–£120 UK |
| Personal loan lead | CPL (form submit) | £15–£60 UK |
| Funded loan | CPA | £80–£300+ UK |

*Rates vary wildly by credit tier, geo, and cap. Always confirm in IO — never quote from this table to partners.*
