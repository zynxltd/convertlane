# Managing Director — role playbook

**Accountable for:** Network direction, escalations, commercial exceptions, key relationships.

**Backup when away:** Compliance Lead (no new approvals without Compliance).

---

## You own

- Final say on rate exceptions >10%, credit lines, and reinstatements after fraud  
- P1 incidents and regulator/media contact  
- Policy approval (with Compliance drafting)  
- Key advertiser / publisher relationships  
- Cash risk: never promise publisher pay without funded advertiser exposure  

---

## Daily (10–15 min)

- [ ] Scan P1 inbox / Slack  
- [ ] Glance compliance queue — anything stuck >5 days?  
- [ ] One pipeline action (call, close, or unblock)  

---

## Weekly

- [ ] Lead Monday stand-up ([17-ops-calendar.md](../17-ops-calendar.md))  
- [ ] Review Finance cash waterfall — float OK?  
- [ ] Review rejected/held applications — pattern?  

---

## Monthly

- [ ] Sign off large payout batches if > internal threshold  
- [ ] Review metrics: approval time, fraud %, on-time pay %  
- [ ] 30 min with Compliance — policy gaps?  

---

## Handoffs

| From | You do |
|------|--------|
| Compliance | Decide enhanced DD exceptions (score 60+ rare) |
| Finance | Approve credit line not covered by policy |
| AM | Approve major rate/cap changes |
| Anyone | P1 escalation within 4 hours |

| To | You hand off |
|----|----------------|
| Compliance | All DD execution — you do not skip gates |
| AMs | Day-to-day partner comms |
| Finance | All money movement |

---

## If this → then that

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| AM wants to approve partner without Compliance | **No** — refer to gates |
| Advertiser wants to go live today, no prepay | **No** — Finance gate |
| Publisher threatens legal over payout | Loop Finance + Compliance; no admission of liability |
| Competitor poaching top publisher | Publisher Lead owns retention; you join if strategic |
| Compliance on leave | No new `approved` statuses — holds only |

---

## Tools

- Compliance portal (read-only when needed)  
- Affise dashboard (overview)  
- Finance ledger summary  
- partners@ email (escalations only — do not become inbox default)  

---

## Success looks like

- Zero live partners without DD approval  
- Zero publisher pay runs without funded liability  
- Decisions documented in audit log, not WhatsApp only
