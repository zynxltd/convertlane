# Publisher Partnerships Lead — role playbook

**Accountable for:** Publisher pipeline, AM service, publisher IOs, traffic quality (with Compliance).

**Backup:** MD.

---

## You own

- Publisher sales and applications quality pre-screen  
- Publisher IOs ([07-io](../07-insertion-order-template.md)) aligned with commercial promises  
- Day-to-day publisher comms (rates, caps, creatives)  
- Traffic quality — first line before Compliance fraud ruling  
- Publisher probation (first 30 days)  
- Matching publishers to offers on `/offers`  

---

## Daily (30–45 min)

- [ ] Publisher inbox — SLA 4 business hours median  
- [ ] Affise: cap warnings, CR anomalies for your accounts  
- [ ] New `approved` publishers — schedule IO + welcome email  
- [ ] `/offers` — know what’s open for recruitment  

---

## Per publisher journey

| Stage | You do |
|-------|--------|
| Apply received | Optional pre-call; don’t promise approval |
| DD in progress | Support docs; no rates promised |
| Approved | Send agreement + IO per offer; Affise welcome **after** Ops setup |
| Live | Weekly stats first month; creative approvals to Compliance |
| Issue | Gather logs → Compliance for fraud decision |
| Exit | Support offboarding comms with Compliance |

---

## Weekly

- [ ] Cap utilisation per top 10 publishers  
- [ ] Probation publishers review  
- [ ] Coordinate Wed fraud call with Compliance  

---

## Monthly

- [ ] Top publisher performance summary to MD  
- [ ] Identify 2 recruitment targets for open offers  

---

## Handoffs

| To | When |
|----|------|
| Compliance | Every new app; fraud suspects; creative approval |
| Finance & Ops | IO signed → request Affise partner + offer assignment |
| Advertiser Lead | Publisher needs offer terms clarification |
| MD | Strategic pub or rate exception >10% |

---

## If this → then that

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Publisher wants higher rate | IO amendment or reject — no verbal caps |
| Brand bidding on finance offer | Stop traffic; Compliance loop |
| Cap hit mid-day | Pause in Affise; don’t promise over-cap pay |
| Publisher asks for other pubs’ rates | Confidential — decline |
| New sub-ID spike | Ask source; flag Compliance if suspicious |

---

## Tools

- Affise publisher panel  
- `/compliance` (read application)  
- `/offers` for catalogue  
- CRM/spreadsheet pipeline (optional)  

---

## Do not do

- Approve DD or change compliance status  
- Promise payout dates outside [08-payout-policy](../08-publisher-payout-policy.md)  
- Add publisher to offer without Compliance `approved` + signed IO  

---

## Success looks like

- Publishers know their AM by name  
- IO always matches Affise settings  
- Fraud flagged early, not at payout
