# Advertiser Sales Lead — role playbook

**Accountable for:** Advertiser pipeline, offer commercial terms, advertiser IOs, offer briefs to Ops.

**Backup:** MD.

---

## You own

- Advertiser acquisition and application quality  
- Commercial terms on each offer (rate, cap, geo, traffic rules)  
- Advertiser IOs + offer brief for [14-launch](../14-offer-launch-checklist.md)  
- Advertiser relationship and performance reporting  
- Ensuring landing pages / postbacks work with advertiser tech  
- Coordination with Publisher Lead on inventory needs  

---

## Daily (30–45 min)

- [ ] Advertiser inbox — SLA 4 business hours  
- [ ] Cap vs delivery on live offers  
- [ ] Pipeline: prepay pending, launches scheduled  
- [ ] Postback / validation issues — chase advertiser same day  

---

## Per advertiser journey

| Stage | You do |
|-------|--------|
| Apply | Qualify lead; don’t promise go-live date |
| DD | Supply licence/product proof; coordinate Finance on prepay |
| Approved + funded | Draft IO; brief Ops for Affise |
| Live | Daily/weekly perf email to advertiser |
| Dispute | Pull Affise IDs; Finance reconciles |
| Non-pay | Alert Finance immediately — caps pause |

---

## New offer launch (your checklist)

- [ ] Commercial terms agreed in writing  
- [ ] IO drafted and signed  
- [ ] Compliance cleared vertical/geo  
- [ ] Finance confirmed prepay/credit slice  
- [ ] Offer brief to Finance & Ops (URLs, postback, caps)  
- [ ] [14-offer-launch-checklist](../14-offer-launch-checklist.md) signed  
- [ ] Publisher Lead briefed on recruitment  

---

## Weekly

- [ ] Advertiser cap review  
- [ ] Prepay burn rate — warn Finance at 75%  
- [ ] IO expiries in next 30 days  

---

## Handoffs

| To | When |
|----|------|
| Compliance | Applications, licences, product claims |
| Finance | Prepay invoices, credit requests, disputes |
| Finance & Ops | Signed IO + offer brief for Affise |
| Publisher Lead | Inventory needs, private offers |
| MD | Major pricing or credit exceptions |

---

## If this → then that

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Advertiser wants live without prepay | Finance gate — no launch |
| Advertiser rejects many conversions | Pull validation rules; 5-day SLA |
| Need more volume | Publisher Lead recruits — you don’t unvet pubs |
| Rate change mid-month | New IO or amendment before Affise change |
| Licence expiring | Compliance + pause geos |

---

## Tools

- Affise advertiser reports  
- `/compliance`  
- Ledger view (Finance shared) for prepay balance  
- `/offers` public catalogue (optional listing)  

---

## Do not do

- Approve DD or sanctions  
- Open Affise caps without Finance funding confirmation  
- Promise publisher volume numbers  

---

## Success looks like

- Every live offer has signed IO + funded ledger line  
- Advertisers get stats on schedule  
- Postbacks validated within agreed SLA
