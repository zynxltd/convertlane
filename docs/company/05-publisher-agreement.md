## 1. Appointment

1.1 ConvertLane operates a performance marketing network. Upon approval, we appoint you on a **non-exclusive** basis to promote **approved offers** listed in the Affise panel or separate IO.

1.2 This Agreement + each signed **Insertion Order (IO)** form the contract. If IO conflicts with this Agreement, **IO prevails** for that offer.

1.3 No tracking links until ConvertLane sets your status to **approved** in writing.

---

## 2. Your obligations

You will:

- (a) Promote only with **approved links, creatives, and landing pages**;  
- (b) Comply with all applicable laws (advertising, privacy, consumer protection);  
- (c) Make clear **affiliate disclosures** where required;  
- (d) Send only **opt-in email** where email traffic is permitted;  
- (e) **Not** use prohibited traffic (incent, brand bidding, bots, etc.) unless IO allows;  
- (f) **Not** rebroker or resell your tracking links without written consent;  
- (g) Provide accurate company and payment information;  
- (h) Notify us within **24 hours** of any fraud suspicion or security incident affecting traffic;  
- (i) Cooperate with compliance and quality audits.

Full traffic rules: ConvertLane Traffic & Creative Policy (provided on approval).

---

## 3. Compliance & KYC

3.1 You completed our due diligence pack truthfully. You must notify us within **5 business days** of any change in directors, UBOs (≥25%), address, or bank details.

3.2 We may re-screen sanctions/PEP at any time. We may suspend or terminate immediately for false information.

---

## 4. Compensation

4.1 Payout per IO (CPA, CPL, CPS, or hybrid).

4.2 **Default payment terms** (unless IO states otherwise):

| Term | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Cycle | Net-30 |
| Pay date | 15th of month for prior calendar month |
| Threshold | £100 / $100 equivalent |
| Method | Wise Business or agreed alternative |

4.3 We pay **approved conversions** only, per Affise and advertiser validation. We may **deduct** invalid conversions, chargebacks, and prior overpayments.

4.4 Tax: You are responsible for your own taxes. We may require tax forms before first payout.

4.5 We are **not** liable for advertiser non-payment beyond our obligation to pay you only from funds properly allocated to your approved conversions under our policies.

---

## 5. Confidentiality

Offer rates, caps, advertiser identities (where marked private), and unpublished IO terms are **confidential**.

---

## 6. Intellectual property

Advertisers grant rights to use creatives only for promotion during the term. You grant us a licence to use your logos and case studies for marketing with prior consent.

---

## 7. Term & termination

7.1 Continues until terminated.

7.2 Either party: **7 days’ written notice** for convenience.

7.3 We may terminate **immediately** for material breach, fraud, policy violation, or regulatory risk.

7.4 On termination: links deactivated; final payout per policy for approved conversions before cut-off.

---

## 8. Indemnity

You indemnify ConvertLane against claims arising from your traffic, creatives, data practices, or breach of law — except where caused by our gross negligence.

---

## 9. Liability

9.1 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

9.2 Our total liability capped at **fees paid to you in the 3 months** before the claim (except fraud or death/personal injury).
