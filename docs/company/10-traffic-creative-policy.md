# Traffic & creative policy

**Owner:** Head of Compliance · **Review:** Annual

Applies to all publishers unless a signed IO states otherwise.

---

## Allowed traffic (subject to IO)

| Source | Notes |
|--------|--------|
| SEO / content sites | Original content; no scraped trademark sites |
| Paid search | Non-brand unless IO permits brand |
| Paid social | Platform-compliant; targeting per geo rules |
| Native | Pre-lander approval required |
| Email | **Opt-in only** — proof on request |
| Display / programmatic | Brand-safe placements |
| Influencer | Disclosure required |
| Coupon / cashback | Advertiser approval required in IO |

**Default:** If not listed in IO, it is **not allowed**.

---

## Prohibited (unless IO explicitly permits)

- Incentivised traffic (rewards, points for sign-up)  
- Brand bidding / trademark PPC  
- Adult traffic on non-adult offers  
- Malware, pop-unders, forced redirects  
- Cookie stuffing / toolbars  
- Bot or emulated device traffic  
- Spam email (non opt-in)  
- Misleading claims (false earnings, fake urgency)  
- Sub-ID resale / rebrokering without written consent  

---

## Creatives & landers

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Approval | All new landers and major creative changes pre-approved |
| Submit via | Email to AM or compliance ticket — include final URL |
| SLA | 2 business days standard; 24h for urgent (Compliance discretion) |
| Archive | Save approved screenshot/PDF with date |
| Localisation | Must match allowed geo language and law |

---

## Disclosures

Publishers must clearly disclose affiliate relationship:

- “Advertisement” / “Sponsored” / “Affiliate link” as required by jurisdiction  
- UK: CAP Code awareness for finance and health claims  
- US: FTC endorsement guides  

---

## Sub-IDs

- Publisher may use sub-IDs for tracking  
- ConvertLane may pause individual sub-IDs for quality issues  
- Publisher must not rotate banned sub-IDs to evade blocks  

---

## Brand bidding

| IO says | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Silent | **No** brand bidding |
| “Brand allowed” | Only listed terms and match types |
| Violation | Warning → conversion reject → terminate |

---

## Monitoring

- Weekly cap and CR review by AM  
- Affise fraud flags reviewed Wednesdays  
- Advertiser complaints triaged within 1 business day
