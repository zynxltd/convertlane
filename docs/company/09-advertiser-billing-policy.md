## Summary

Advertisers fund campaigns **before or during** delivery. ConvertLane does not carry unlimited credit risk.

| Model | When to use |
|-------|-------------|
| **Prepay** | Default — all new advertisers |
| **Credit line** | Established advertisers only — Finance + MD approval |
| **Hybrid** | Prepay for new geos; credit for proven geos |

---

## Prepay (default)

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| Minimum top-up | £5,000 / $5,000 / €5,000 equivalent (or IO minimum) |
| When due | Before offer goes live **and** before cap increase |
| Method | Bank transfer to ConvertLane business account or Wise inbound |
| Allocation | Linked to offer ID in ledger spreadsheet |
| Low balance | Pause caps at **90%** spend; hard pause at **100%** |

Finance sends **top-up reminder** at 75% consumed.

---

## Credit line (optional)

Requires:

- Completed [credit application](templates/credit-application-advertiser.md)  
- 6+ months clean history with ConvertLane **or** strong external references  
- Approval: Finance **+** Compliance **+** MD  
- Signed credit addendum to [advertiser agreement](06-advertiser-agreement.md)  

| Term | Typical |
|------|---------|
| Limit | £10k–£100k (Finance sets) |
| Payment terms | Net-15 or Net-30 on invoice |
| Interest | Not charged if paid on time; late fee 2% monthly on overdue (if legal) |
| Review | Monthly exposure check |

**Pause traffic** immediately if invoice **15+ days** overdue.

---

## Invoicing

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Frequency | Monthly in arrears (credit) or on prepay top-up (receipt) |
| Content | Offer ID, conversions, rate, adjustments |
| VAT | Per UK rules and advertiser VAT status |
| Send to | Billing contact on DD file |
| Due | Net-15 (credit) unless IO states otherwise |

---

## Non-payment

| Days overdue | Action |
|--------------|--------|
| 7 | Reminder email |
| 14 | Pause all caps |
| 21 | Compliance + MD review; legal notice |
| 30+ | Terminate; withhold publisher pay until funded (see internal cash policy) |

**Never pay publishers** from unfunded advertiser spend without MD written exception.

---

## Refunds to advertiser

- Invalid traffic confirmed → credit note  
- Overpayment → refund within **10 business days**  
- Document in ledger with Affise export attached  

---

## Publisher payment linkage

```
Advertiser funds → ConvertLane account → Publisher liability accrued → Publisher paid on 15th
```

Finance maintains simple **cash waterfall** spreadsheet monthly.

---

## Records

- Invoice PDF, payment proof, IO reference  
- Retain **7 years**  
- Match Affise advertiser stats monthly
