# Advertiser credit & cash-flow procedure

**Owner:** Finance & Operations · **Co-owner:** Head of Compliance · **Review:** Annual

**Related:** [09-advertiser-billing-policy.md](09-advertiser-billing-policy.md) · [templates/credit-application-advertiser.md](templates/credit-application-advertiser.md) · [19-chargebacks-and-clawbacks.md](19-chargebacks-and-clawbacks.md) · [advertiser-checklist](../due-diligence/advertiser-checklist.md) (section C)

---

## Purpose

Let advertisers pay on **invoice (credit)** only when ConvertLane can afford the exposure — and **never pay publishers** from money we have not collected or safely reserved.

**Default for all new advertisers:** prepay. Credit is the exception.

---

## Where this sits in the process

```
Apply → DD pack → Finance credit/prepay decision → Compliance approve → IO signed
     → [PREPAY RECEIVED or CREDIT LIMIT SIGNED] → Affise live → monthly invoice/settlement
```

**Gate:** No Affise cap > 0 until Finance records **funding status** on DD pack + ledger.

---

## Step 1 — Decide prepay vs credit (Day 0–5 of DD)

| Situation | Decision |
|-----------|----------|
| New advertiser (< 2 years trading) | **Prepay only** — first 90 days minimum |
| Risk score ≥ 40 | **Prepay only** until 90 days clean delivery |
| Any prior payment dispute with ConvertLane | **Prepay + reserve** |
| Credit check: CCJ, winding-up, negative net assets | **Prepay only** or **reject** |
| Established advertiser, clean history 6+ months | **May apply for credit** |
| MD strategic account | Credit possible with MD + Finance written approval |

Finance completes [credit application](templates/credit-application-advertiser.md) for credit requests only.

---

## Step 2 — Credit application workflow

| Step | Who | Action | Record |
|------|-----|--------|--------|
| 1 | Advertiser Lead | Sends credit application form + signed **credit check authorisation** | Email + DD folder |
| 2 | Finance | Order **third-party business credit report** (see below) | PDF in DD folder |
| 3 | Finance | Calculate **exposure limit** (formula below) | DD pack + compliance UI `exposure_limit_gbp` |
| 4 | Finance | Propose terms: limit, Net-15/30, review date | Credit application form |
| 5 | Compliance | Confirm no sanctions/regulatory block | Sign-off on form |
| 6 | MD | Approve if limit > £25k or terms non-standard | Signature on form |
| 7 | Finance | Issue **credit schedule** (addendum to advertiser agreement) | Signed PDF |
| 8 | Finance | Create ledger row: limit, used, available | Cash waterfall spreadsheet |

**Without steps 7–8:** treat as prepay only.

---

## Step 3 — Exposure limit (how much credit to grant)

**Formula (simple):**

```
Exposure limit = MAX expected monthly publisher payout liability × 1.5
```

| Input | Source |
|-------|--------|
| Expected monthly liability | IO cap × payout × realistic conversion rate (AM estimate) |
| 1.5 multiplier | Buffer for growth + dispute window |

**Caps:**

| Band | Max unsecured limit (guideline) |
|------|-------------------------------|
| First credit grant | £10,000–£25,000 |
| Proven 6 months clean | Up to £50,000 |
| Above £50,000 | MD approval required |

Reduce limit if: high invalid rate, regulated vertical stress, or thin balance sheet on credit report.

## Step 4 — Third-party tools (credit & KYB)

| Check | Third party? | Typical providers | Who runs |
|-------|----------------|-------------------|----------|
| **Business credit report** | **Yes** | Creditsafe, Experian Business, Dun & Bradstreet | Finance |
| **Sanctions / PEP** | **Yes** | ComplyAdvantage, Refinitiv World-Check, OFSI list | Compliance |
| **Company registry** | **Yes** (official) | Companies House (UK), local registries | Compliance |
| **Risk score (0–100)** | **No — internal** | [risk-scoring-matrix](../due-diligence/risk-scoring-matrix.md) | Compliance |
| **Exposure limit** | **No — internal calc** | This procedure | Finance |

Risk score is **not** auto-generated by credit bureau — Compliance scores manually per matrix, then enters in `/compliance`.

Guide: [risk-scoring-guide.md](../due-diligence/risk-scoring-guide.md).

## Step 5 — Prepay process (default path)

| Step | Who | Action |
|------|-----|--------|
| 1 | Advertiser Lead | Confirm IO amount needed for launch + 30 days |
| 2 | Finance | Send invoice or bank details |
| 3 | Advertiser | Wire to ConvertLane Wise/bank |
| 4 | Finance | Match reference → ledger **prepay in** → allocate to **offer ID** |
| 5 | Finance | Set Affise cap only after cleared funds |
| 6 | Finance | Monitor burn: reminder at **75%**, pause at **90%**, hard stop **100%** |

**Ledger columns:** Date | Advertiser | Offer ID | Prepay in | Allocated | Remaining balance

---

## Step 6 — Credit account monitoring (monthly)

| Check | Owner | Trigger |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Outstanding invoice vs limit | Finance | Monthly |
| Age of debt | Finance | >7 days reminder, >14 pause, >21 escalate |
| Publisher liability vs collected funds | Finance | Before every 15th payout |
| Limit review date | Finance | Every 90 days on credit form |

**Pause all caps immediately** if:

- Invoice overdue **14+ days**
- Exposure used > approved limit
- Compliance fraud hold on advertiser
- Credit report new CCJ / insolvency filing

## Cash-flow rule (protect publishers and ConvertLane)

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  RULE: Publisher payout £ only if:                      │
│  (a) Conversions approved in Affise, AND                │
│  (b) Advertiser prepay balance OR credit headroom covers  │
│      that offer's publisher liability, AND              │
│  (c) No open fraud hold, AND                            │
│  (d) Not past chargeback/reversal window without reserve  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

| Scenario | Action |
|----------|--------|
| Credit advertiser, invoice not yet due | Pay publishers only up to **collected prepay + approved credit headroom** for that offer |
| Credit advertiser, invoice overdue 14d | **Pause caps**; do not pay publishers for new conversions on that offer |
| Prepay exhausted | Pause caps; top-up before new traffic |
| High chargeback rate on offer | Hold publisher payout slice per [19-chargebacks](19-chargebacks-and-clawbacks.md) |

**MD written exception only** if paying publishers ahead of collection.

---

## Step 7 — Chargebacks & reversals

Advertiser disputes and chargebacks are **not** the same as credit terms — but they affect cash.

Full procedure: **[19-chargebacks-and-clawbacks.md](19-chargebacks-and-clawbacks.md)**

Summary:

- IO defines **reversal window** (e.g. 30 days)  
- Invalid conversions rejected in Affise → not paid to publisher  
- Advertiser chargebacks after publisher paid → clawback publisher or network absorbs per IO/agreement
