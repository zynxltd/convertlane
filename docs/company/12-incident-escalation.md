# Incident & escalation

**Owner:** Managing Director · **Review:** Annual

---

## Severity levels

| Level | Examples | Response time |
|-------|----------|---------------|
| **P1** | Sanctions hit on active partner; data breach; advertiser prepay missing but traffic live | **Immediate** (same day) |
| **P2** | Fraud spike > 20% invalid; cap blown 200%; licence expiry | **4 business hours** |
| **P3** | Creative complaint; single publisher dispute; IO typo | **2 business days** |
| **P4** | General enquiry | Normal AM SLA |

---

## Escalation path

```
Reporter → Owning AM → Compliance (if fraud/regulatory) → Finance (if money) → MD (P1 / legal)
```

| Situation | First call | Decision maker |
|-----------|------------|----------------|
| Fraud allegation | Compliance | Compliance |
| Payout hold | Finance | Finance + Compliance |
| Advertiser non-pay | Finance | Finance → MD |
| Partner termination | Compliance | Compliance |
| Media / legal threat | MD | MD + external solicitor |
| Regulator enquiry | MD | MD + Compliance + solicitor |

## Communication templates

| Audience | Who sends | Tone |
|----------|-----------|------|
| Advertiser (fraud) | Advertiser Lead + Compliance cc | Factual, conversion IDs attached |
| Publisher (fraud) | Publisher Lead | Clear breach, evidence, next steps |
| Publisher (payout delay) | Finance | Date certain or honest uncertainty |
| All-hands internal | MD | Short facts, owners, ETA |

Do not admit legal liability in email — “we are investigating.”

---

## Post-incident

Within **5 business days** of P1/P2 close:

- One-page summary: what happened, root cause, fix, owner  
- File in `/docs/incidents/YYYY-MM-title.md` (create folder as needed)  
- Update policy/SOP if gap found
