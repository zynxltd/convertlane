# Partner offboarding

**Owner:** Head of Compliance · **Review:** Annual

Detailed reject/exit SOP: [rejection-and-offboarding](../due-diligence/rejection-and-offboarding.md).

---

## When we offboard

| Reason | Examples |
|--------|----------|
| **For cause** | Fraud, policy breach, fake documents |
| **Commercial** | Advertiser ended offer; publisher inactive 12 months |
| **Risk** | Sanctions hit; licence revoked |
| **Mutual** | Partner request to close |

---

## Steps (all offboards)

| # | Action | Owner | Timing |
|---|--------|-------|--------|
| 1 | Set status `offboarded` in compliance UI | Compliance | Day 0 |
| 2 | Disable Affise partner / offer access | Finance & Ops | Day 0 |
| 3 | Email partner with reason (for-cause: factual, no insults) | AM + Compliance cc | Day 0–1 |
| 4 | Final conversion cut-off date documented | Finance | Day 0 |
| 5 | Final payout or withholding letter | Finance | Per [08-publisher-payout-policy.md](08-publisher-payout-policy.md) |
| 6 | Archive DD pack (do not delete early) | Compliance | Day 0 |
| 7 | Remove from active AM pipeline | AM | Day 0 |

---

## Publisher final payout

| Scenario | Payout |
|----------|--------|
| Clean exit | Pay approved conversions through cut-off date on normal cycle |
| Fraud proven | Withhold pending investigation; document IDs |
| Dispute | Hold only disputed portion |

---

## Advertiser exit

- Unused prepay refunded within **10 business days** (minus contractual fees if any)  
- Final invoice for credit accounts  
- Confirm caps zero in Affise  

---

## Reinstatement

- **Rejected** applicants: new application only with **new entity** — Compliance Lead approval  
- **Offboarded for cause:** No reinstatement unless MD + Compliance written exception  

---

## Internal list

Maintain **internal blocklist** (company reg numbers, domains, UBO names) — Compliance owns; never share externally.
