# Publisher payout policy

**Owner:** Finance & Operations · **Review:** Annual

---

## Summary

| Item | Standard term |
|------|----------------|
| Cycle | **Net-30** |
| Payment date | **15th** of month for **prior calendar month** |
| Minimum threshold | **£100** or **$100** equivalent |
| Method | **Wise Business** (primary) |
| Currencies | GBP, USD, EUR (as agreed in IO) |
| FX | Mid-market Wise rate; fees disclosed on request |

---

## Eligibility for payout

Publisher must be:

- Status `approved` in compliance system  
- Signed [publisher agreement](05-publisher-agreement.md)  
- Valid IO for each offer paid  
- Payment details on file ([payment details template](templates/publisher-payment-details.md))  
- No open fraud investigation hold  
- No outstanding clawback dispute unresolved  

---

## What we pay

- **Approved** conversions per Affise, matching IO definition  
- Minus invalid / rejected conversions  
- Minus prior-period adjustments agreed in writing  
- Minus chargebacks where IO allows advertiser rejection  

---

## What we do not pay

- Conversions before approval date  
- Geo-invalid or cap-exceeded (unless IO amended)  
- Traffic from prohibited sources per IO  
- Conversions under active fraud investigation (held until cleared)  

---

## Monthly process

| Day (approx) | Task | Owner |
|--------------|------|-------|
| 1st–3rd | Export prior month stats from Affise | Finance & Ops |
| 3rd–5th | Reconcile with advertiser-validated numbers | Finance & Ops |
| 5th–7th | Apply deductions; Compliance clears holds | Finance + Compliance |
| 7th–10th | Prepare Wise batch; MD spot-check if > £50k total | Finance |
| **15th** | Execute transfers | Finance |
| 15th+ | Send remittance advice email per publisher | Finance |

If **15th** is non-business day, pay **next business day**.

---

## New publishers (probation)

- First payout may be held until **30 days** clean delivery if Compliance requests  
- Lower caps during probation — documented in IO  

---

## Tax

- Publisher responsible for own tax  
- Collect W-8BEN / W-9 or UK tax declaration as applicable  
- Issue annual summary on request (not formal tax advice)  

---

## Disputes

- Publisher raises within **14 days** of remittance  
- Finance re-pulls Affise; AM comments  
- Compliance decides fraud-related withholds  
- Resolution within **10 business days**  

---

## Failure to pay (internal)

If ConvertLane cannot pay on schedule:

- MD notifies affected publishers within **2 business days**  
- Document reason (advertiser non-pay, bank issue)  
- Never promise payout dates not backed by collected advertiser funds  

**Best practice:** Maintain **publisher reserve** = 1 month average publisher liability where cash flow allows.
