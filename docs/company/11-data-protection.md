# Data protection (GDPR summary)

**Owner:** Head of Compliance · **Review:** Annual

Public privacy policy: website `/privacy`. This is the **internal** operating summary.

---

## What data we hold

| Category | Examples | Lawful basis |
|----------|----------|--------------|
| Partner business data | Company name, registry, VAT | Contract + legal obligation |
| KYC data | ID copies, UBO forms | Legal obligation + legitimate interest |
| Contact data | Email, phone, AM notes | Contract |
| Traffic / performance | Clicks, conversions (Affise) | Contract |
| Financial | Bank details, invoices | Contract + legal obligation |
| Website | Cookies, analytics | Consent / legitimate interest |

---

## Principles (keep it simple)

1. Collect only what DD and operations need.  
2. Store in **encrypted** locations — no ID docs in Slack.  
3. Limit access to Compliance, Finance, and owning AM.  
4. Delete or anonymise when retention period ends.  
5. Honour **access / deletion** requests within 30 days (unless legal hold).  

---

## Breach response (72-hour rule)

| Hour | Action |
|------|--------|
| 0–4 | Contain (rotate keys, disable access) |
| 4–24 | Assess scope; Compliance documents |
| 24–72 | ICO notification if required; notify partners if high risk |
| 72+ | Remediation plan; post-incident review |

Escalation: [12-incident-escalation.md](12-incident-escalation.md).

---

## International transfers

- Prefer UK/EEA processors  
- If US tools used, ensure appropriate safeguards (SCCs / UK IDTA)  
- Document in processor register
