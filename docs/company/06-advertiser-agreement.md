## 1. Services

1.1 ConvertLane will distribute your **approved offers** to vetted publishers via the Affise platform, subject to this Agreement and each **Insertion Order (IO)**.

1.2 We provide: publisher recruitment (within our standards), tracking, cap management, reporting, and payment collection per our billing policy.

1.3 We do **not** guarantee volume, ranking, or specific publishers.

---

## 2. Your obligations

You will:

- (a) Provide accurate offer terms, landing pages, postbacks, and validation rules;  
- (b) Maintain valid licences for regulated products (finance, gambling, health, etc.);  
- (c) **Fund** campaigns via prepay or approved credit before traffic scales;  
- (d) Validate conversions in good faith within agreed SLAs;  
- (e) Approve or reject conversions with documented reasons;  
- (f) Not request traffic that violates law or our policies;  
- (g) Keep confidential publisher identities where we mark them private;  
- (h) Notify us of material product or licence changes within **5 business days**.

---

## 3. Compliance

3.1 You completed our advertiser due diligence pack accurately.

3.2 You indemnify ConvertLane for claims arising from your product, creative claims, or lack of licence — except our gross negligence.

3.3 We may pause or remove offers that create regulatory or fraud risk.

---

## 4. Fees & payment

4.1 You pay ConvertLane per IO: typically **payout to publisher + network fee** (margin defined in IO or rate card).

4.2 **Default billing:**

| Model | Terms |
|-------|--------|
| Prepay | Funds received before caps open |
| Credit | Only with signed credit addendum — Net-15/30 invoice |

4.3 Unused prepay refunded on termination minus earned fees and contractual adjustments.

4.4 Late payment: we may **pause caps** immediately; interest may apply on credit accounts per credit addendum.

Full policy: [09-advertiser-billing-policy.md](09-advertiser-billing-policy.md).

---

## 5. Tracking & validation

5.1 Affise is the system of record unless IO specifies otherwise.

5.2 You will implement postbacks or provide validation files within **3 business days** of month end unless IO differs.

5.3 Disputes on conversions: raised within **14 days** with specific IDs; we investigate with publishers.

---

## 6. Fraud & quality

6.1 We operate anti-fraud measures but do not warrant zero fraud.

6.2 Either party may reject invalid conversions per IO definition.

6.3 Material fraud: we may pause offer and notify you; you support investigation.

---

## 7. Confidentiality & data

Business terms, unpublished rates, and publisher lists (where private) are confidential.

Each party complies with applicable data protection law. DPA signed separately if required.

---

## 8. Term & termination

8.1 Until terminated.

8.2 Either party: **14 days’ notice** for convenience (active IOs wind down).

8.3 Immediate termination for breach, fraud, insolvency, or licence loss.

8.4 On termination: caps zeroed; final reconciliation within **20 business days**.

---

## 9. Liability

9.1 No indirect/consequential liability.

9.2 Cap: **fees paid by Advertiser to ConvertLane in prior 3 months**, except fraud, death, personal injury.
