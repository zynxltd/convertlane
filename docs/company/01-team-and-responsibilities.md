## RACI legend

- **R** = Responsible (does the work)  
- **A** = Accountable (single decision owner)  
- **C** = Consulted  
- **I** = Informed

## Sign-off requirements

| Decision | Required approvers |
|----------|------------------|
| Publisher **approved** | Compliance **+** Publisher Lead |
| Advertiser **approved** | Compliance **+** Advertiser Lead **+** Finance (credit/prepay) |
| Enhanced DD (high risk) | Compliance Lead only (MD informed) |
| **Reject** partner | Compliance Lead (notes mandatory) |
| IO rate change > 10% | AM who owns account **+** MD |
| Open cap above IO | Compliance **+** owning AM |
| Publisher payout release | Finance (Compliance flag check) |
| Advertiser on credit (not prepay) | Finance **+** Compliance **+** MD |

## Weekly rhythm (30-minute stand-up)

| Day | Topic |
|-----|--------|
| Monday | Pipeline: applications in review, offers launching this week |
| Wednesday | Fraud / quality flags, cap utilisation |
| Friday | Payout prep (if month-end), overdue advertiser invoices |

**Output:** Three bullet actions per person in shared task list (Notion, Linear, or spreadsheet).

## Out-of-office
