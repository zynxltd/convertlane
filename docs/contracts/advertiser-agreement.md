## 1. Services

1.1 ConvertLane will distribute your **approved offers** to vetted affiliates via the Offer18 platform (`convertlane.offer18.com`), subject to this Agreement and each signed **Insertion Order (IO)**.

1.2 We provide: affiliate recruitment (within our vetting standards), tracking via Offer18, cap management, reporting, and billing collection per our billing policy.

1.3 We do **not** guarantee volume, specific affiliates, or placement.

---

## 2. Your obligations

You will:

- (a) Provide accurate offer terms, landing pages, postbacks/pixels, and conversion validation rules;
- (b) Maintain valid licences and permissions for regulated products (FCA authorisation, credit broking permissions, consumer credit licences where applicable);
- (c) **Fund** campaigns via prepay before traffic scales, unless ConvertLane approves credit in writing;
- (d) Validate conversions in good faith within the SLA in each IO;
- (e) Approve or reject conversions with documented reasons where requested;
- (f) Not request traffic that violates law or our traffic policy;
- (g) Keep affiliate identities confidential where we mark them private;
- (h) Notify us within **5 business days** of material product, licence, or landing page changes.

---

## 3. Compliance

3.1 You completed our advertiser due diligence pack accurately.

3.2 You indemnify ConvertLane for claims arising from your product, advertising claims, or lack of licence — except our gross negligence.

3.3 We may pause or remove offers that create regulatory, fraud, or reputational risk without liability.

3.4 **Finance vertical:** You confirm all loan, credit card, and credit-score offers comply with UK financial promotion rules. You are solely responsible for FCA status and fair-lending compliance on your landing pages.

---

## 4. Fees & payment

4.1 You pay ConvertLane per IO: typically **affiliate payout + network fee** (margin defined in IO).

4.2 **Default billing:**

| Model | Terms |
|-------|--------|
| Prepay | Funds received and confirmed before caps open |
| Credit | Only with signed credit addendum — Net-15 or Net-30 invoice |

4.3 Unused prepay refunded on termination minus earned fees and contractual adjustments.

4.4 Late payment: we may **pause caps immediately**; interest may apply on credit accounts per credit addendum.

4.5 Bank details for prepay provided separately by ConvertLane Finance.

---

## 5. Tracking & validation

5.1 **Offer18** is the system of record for clicks, conversions, and caps unless the IO specifies otherwise.

5.2 You will implement postbacks or provide validation files within **3 business days** of month end unless the IO differs.

5.3 Disputes on conversions: raised within **14 days** with specific conversion IDs; we investigate with affiliates.

---

## 6. Fraud & quality

6.1 We operate basic quality controls; advanced fraud rules may be added later by agreement.

6.2 Either party may reject invalid conversions per IO definition.

6.3 Material fraud: we may pause the offer and notify you; you support investigation.

---

## 7. Confidentiality & data

Business terms, unpublished rates, and affiliate lists (where private) are confidential.

Each party complies with UK GDPR. DPA signed separately if required.

---

## 8. Term & termination

8.1 Until terminated by either party.

8.2 Either party: **14 days’ written notice** for convenience (active IOs wind down per IO terms).

8.3 Immediate termination for breach, fraud, insolvency, or licence loss.

8.4 On termination: caps zeroed in Offer18; final reconciliation within **20 business days**.
